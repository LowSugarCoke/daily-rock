# Daily Rock

Systematically listening to rock music, one song a day. Tracking history, ratings, and classic rock evolution.

## Language

**Song**:
A specific musical track of the rock genre, characterized by its title, artist, album, release year, and genre details.
_Avoid_: Track, record, music piece

**Daily Selection**:
The specific song assigned to a particular calendar day for listening and rating.
_Avoid_: Today's song, featured song, daily pick

**Rating**:
An evaluation score and optional text review submitted by a user for a daily selection.
_Avoid_: Score, evaluation, review

**Listening History**:
The chronological list of past daily selections and their associated ratings.
_Avoid_: History list, tracking log, timeline
